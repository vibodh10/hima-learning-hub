# Pearson curriculum and assessment audit

Audit date: 11 August 2026

## Qualification baseline

The website is mapped to the Pearson BTEC Level 3 Nationals in Information Technology (2016 RQF), not the newer 2025 AAQ. The configured programme contains Units 1, 2, 4, 6, 8 and 9.

## Marking integrity

- Objective questions have one valid answer index, unique options and a model answer identical to the keyed correct option.
- Every Unit 2, 4 and 6 practical mark has one explicit rubric point.
- Practical model responses are substantive and accompanied by an evidence checklist and misconception warning.
- Open-ended papers are not finally auto-marked. The full response is retained for Hima, who publishes the bounded final mark and specific feedback.
- Topic-practice written scores remain indicative and are labelled as such; they do not replace Hima's judgement or Pearson summative assessment.
- Fresh practical papers are tested across six consecutive versions for distinct context and stable total marks.

## Important boundaries

- Practice papers are original learning resources. They are not live Pearson papers and do not predict a Pearson grade.
- Unit 2 is a computer-based externally assessed task, set and marked by Pearson. The website rehearsal uses the published 66-mark evidence pattern but must not be used as a replacement for current secure assessment instructions.
- Units 4, 6, 8 and 9 are internally assessed. Website activities are formative preparation; Hima must use the centre's authorised assignment briefs, internal verification and assessment procedures for summative decisions.
